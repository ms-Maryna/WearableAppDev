# CA2 – Presentation Requirements

**Module:** Mobile Integration (2026, Semester 1)

**Programme:** BSc (Hons) in Computing in Software Development

**Weighting:** 15%

**Topic:** 7. Wearable App Development

## Overview

Students work in small groups (3 or 4 members) to research an area of mobile technology and present
their findings to the class. Presentations take place in scheduled class time and are followed by
Q&A and discussion.

## Tasks

1. Research the topic assigned to the group.
2. Prepare a presentation of up to 20 minutes using PowerPoint and/or other suitable presentation
   tools.
3. Include a live demonstration of the technology (for example downloading the SDK, developing or
   running a demo application), showing engagement with the technology at a practical level.
4. Include a References page at the end of the presentation. The research must reference at least 8
   relevant sources.

## Deliverables

1. Presentation delivered to the class group and lecturer.
2. Live demo of the relevant practical aspects of the topic.
3. Answers to questions in a Q&A session after the presentation.
4. Pre-recorded screencast of the live demo, which can be used instead of the live demo if technical
   problems arise.
5. PowerPoint slides with a References page at the end.

All material is uploaded to Moodle. Penalties apply to non-validated late submissions.

## Grading

| Criteria                                                                                                        | Weight |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| Content – relevance and coverage of material, evidence of engagement with the topic, understanding demonstrated | 70%    |
| Organisation and presentation                                                                                   | 30%    |

Group marking applies, except where an individual has not contributed as much as the other members.

### Assessment Criteria

- Quality of explanations and demonstrated understanding
- Completeness
- Depth
- Structure of material and delivery
- Engagement at a practical level
- Responses in the Q&A session
